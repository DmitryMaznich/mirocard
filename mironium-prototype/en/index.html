Home learning without the chaos

Everything for a session — within reach.

Mironium helps parents spend less time searching for and prepping materials, and more time right there with their child, learning together regularly.

Try Mironium How it works

A tablet, a notebook, a pencil, and a little time together.

A screen doesn't replace hands-on learning.

It helps build a skill when the real materials aren't within reach. Mironium gently taps into a child's natural pull toward the tablet: a short useful task — then a small reward the parent chooses.

Less prep

Less searching. More practice.

Topics, tasks, and the order to work through them are already put together in the app — you just open it and start, instead of spending the evening hunting for flashcards.

  • The right topic — in one place, no searching online
  • No printing or storing worksheets
  • The same clear routine every time — easier for a child to settle into

“First find good flashcards. Then print them. Then lay them out…” — sound familiar?

Our position

A gadget is a complement — not a replacement for real play.

A speech therapist reviews and adjusts every topic and how tasks are presented, so materials stay clear and work for different children.

Who's behind Mironium

The idea grew out of one family.

Mironium was created by a parent who was looking for a way to work with their son regularly, without burning out. Every topic is reviewed and adjusted by a speech therapist.
